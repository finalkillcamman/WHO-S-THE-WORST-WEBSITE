02 / EASY TO LEARN

HOW TO PLAY

No hands. No complicated scoring. Just situation cards, color-coded tokens, and a tally board.

01
▤

FLIP & READ

Flip the top situation card and read it out loud to the group.

02
☝

PICK THE FIT

Decide who best fits the situation. Remember: best fit means worst fit!

03
☏

MAKE YOUR CASE

Defend yourself, call out your friends, and try to change some minds.

04
●

VOTE & TALLY

Place your colored token, count the votes, and record them on the tally board.

↻

Tokens go back! After each round, everyone gets their voting tokens back. The tally board keeps the running total.

03 / DEFEND YOURSELF

DON'T JUST
VOTE. DEBATE.

Think the group has it all wrong? Say your piece. Explain why someone else is a better fit. Build your case, defend your reputation, and see if you can turn the whole table.

  • ✓ Make your argument.
  • ✓ Challenge the other side.
  • ✓ Change minds before the vote.

The table can change its mind. Your token casts the vote.

04 / THE FINAL TALLY
THE TALLY BOARD

WHO WILL BE
THE WORST OF THE WORST?

Each round, count the colored tokens in front of each player and record their votes on the tally board. Return the tokens to their owners, flip the next card, and keep the debate rolling.

When the game ends, the player with the most votes earns the crown. Wear it with absolutely no shame.
